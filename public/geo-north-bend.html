HEIGHT LIMITS

• Rear & Side Yards: Maximum 6ft height permitted without variance.

• Front Yard Setback: Maximum 4ft height within 20ft front property setback line.

• Corner Lot Sight Triangle: 3ft max within 30ft intersection triangle.

ENGINEERING & WINDLOAD

• Mount Si Windzone: 90 MPH 3-second gust exposure standard.

• Post Embedment: 24" minimum depth (30" recommended in loose river rock).

• Concrete Footing: 10" diameter bell with drainage rock collar.
