Wind & Exposure Standards in North Bend & Snoqualmie Valley

Properties located in North Bend (ZIP 98045) experience distinct microclimates created by the Cascade Mountain foothills and Mount Si gap winds. Under King County Building Standards, fence structures in exposure B zones are subject to sustained wind loads exceeding 80 mph. To prevent fence blowouts and frost heave, contractors must ensure post footings are excavated to a minimum depth of 24 inches with a gravel drainage base, and horizontal rails must be secured with 10.5–12 gauge exterior-grade stainless steel nails.

Permits & Municipal Setbacks (City of North Bend Municipal Code Title 18)

Under North Bend Municipal Code (NBMC Title 18), residential property line fences up to 6 feet in height do not require a formal city building permit. However, fences constructed within the 15-foot front property setback are restricted to a maximum height of 48 inches (4 feet) to maintain neighborhood street sightlines. Fences on corner lots must strictly adhere to the 25-foot clear vision triangle at intersections, capping fence heights at 36 inches within the intersection corner.

Underground Utilities & Locate Protocol (Washington 811)

Excavating post holes in North Bend requires mandatory notification to Washington 811 (call811.com) at least two business days prior to construction. Due to extensive shallow gas, power, and telecommunication feeds across the valley, homeowners and builders must obtain positive utility markings before setting posts.

Local Guidelines for Homeowners & Fencing Contractors
