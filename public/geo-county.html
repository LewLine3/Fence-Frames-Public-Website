Pacific Northwest Climate & Soil Guidelines (King County)

🌧️ Moisture & Fungal Rot Prevention

King County receives an average of 38 to 65 inches of annual rainfall. Wood fence posts set directly in soil require pressure-treated incised lumber (AWPA UC4A ground-contact grade) to prevent rot. Cedar posts are recommended only when paired with post sleeves or steel pipe brackets (e.g. PostMaster).

❄️ Frost Line & Footing Depth

The standard King County winter frost depth is 12 to 18 inches. Post footings must be excavated to a minimum depth of 24 inches (30 inches for 8ft gates or high-wind zones) with a 4-inch crushed rock drainage base to prevent winter frost heave and water pooling.

🌲 Western Red Cedar Advantage

Western Red Cedar (Thuja plicata) contains natural thujaplicins and tannins that resist Pacific Northwest dampness, moss growth, and insect infestation. Applying a semi-transparent UV-inhibiting stain every 3 to 5 years preserves the natural grain and structural longevity.
