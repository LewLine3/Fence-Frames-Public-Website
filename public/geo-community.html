Step-by-Step Approval Protocol

Si View ARC Submission Process

Follow the official 4-step workflow to guarantee your fence submittal passes committee review.

Step 01

Property Line Survey

Verify pin locations and notify adjacent shared-line property owners before work begins. Corner lots require a 5ft street setback.

Step 02

PayHOA Submittal

Log into payhoa.siviewhoa.info, navigate to "Requests", and attach your generated Fence Frames blueprint takeoff.

Step 03

Bi-Monthly ARC Review

The ARC convenes on the 2nd and 4th Tuesday of each month. Submissions must be uploaded 7 days prior to the meeting date.

Step 04

Build & 60-Day Stain

Once approved, construct according to pre-approved specs and apply the Sherwin-Williams "Asteroid" stain within 60 days.
