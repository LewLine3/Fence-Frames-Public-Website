- Zero developer/engineering jargon (NO "2D CAD", NO "vector canvas").

## 2. Semantic Heading Law (H1 to H6)
- Exactly **ONE** `<h1>` per page.
- `<h2>` for major 3-pillar sections.
- `<h3>` for cards, chapters, and feature tools.
- `<h4>` for sub-panels, takeoff line items.
- `<p>` strictly for body copy in Rowdies Light (300).
